05. Featured Projects

Jun 2026 – Jul 2026

AXI4 Master Bridge · UVM Verification

A parameterizable AXI4 Master Bridge in RTL Verilog that converts a simple user-side streaming interface into burst-based AXI4 transactions, verified with a UVM testbench and SVA.

  • Spans all five AXI4 channels (AW/W/B/AR/R) with INCR, FIXED and WRAP bursts.
  • Splits large transfers so no burst crosses a 4KB boundary; handles unaligned addresses by shifting data and masking WSTRB on the first beat.
  • Read-after-write hazard protection stalls conflicting reads until the write response completes; MAX_OUTSTANDING throttles issued transactions.
  • SVA module bound into the DUT checks handshake stability, FIFO overflow/underflow, 4KB protection and strobe masking.
  • Reached 100% functional coverage (covergroups and crosses); 12 of 15 assertions covered, the rest vacuous.

Clock Domain Crossing

Asynchronous FIFO · Gray-code CDC

A parameterized dual-clock FIFO in Verilog that moves data safely between two unrelated clock domains, following the architecture from Clifford Cummings' SNUG 2002 paper.

  • Only the pointers cross domains: Gray-coded so each step changes one bit, then passed through 2-flop synchronizers.
  • Full and empty are derived from the synchronized Gray pointers; writes are blocked when full and reads when empty.
  • Modular design: fifomem, wptr_full, rptr_empty, sync_r2w, sync_w2r and a top wrapper, with self-checking testbenches.
  • Reworked from an earlier design that crossed binary pointers without proper synchronization.
